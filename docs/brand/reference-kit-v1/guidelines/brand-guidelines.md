# EMPIRE CIGARS — Brand Kit
Version 1.0 | 17 October 2026

## Brand essence
**A collection of empires. A legacy in every puff.**

Empire Cigars is a premium cigar brand inspired by history, craftsmanship, travel, and the visual language of imperial collections. The identity should feel authoritative and collectible, never gaudy or cluttered.

## Logo files
- `logos/empire-cigars-primary.svg` — primary logo, transparent background.
- `logos/empire-cigars-primary-dark.svg` — primary logo on obsidian black.

## Typography
- Display/headlines: **Cormorant Garamond** or **Playfair Display**.
- Body/UI: **Inter** or **Manrope**.
- Use uppercase display typography sparingly with generous letter spacing.
- Prefer sentence case for product descriptions and navigation.

## Visual direction
- Near-black surfaces, subtle tobacco-leaf or paper textures, fine gold rules.
- Product photography should show real cigar craftsmanship, wrapper texture, bands, boxes, and humidor details.
- Use ornament as a frame, not as noise. Keep generous negative space.
- Avoid gradients that look synthetic, excessive glow, generic stock luxury imagery, and inaccurate cigar details.

## Voice
Confident, cultivated, measured, and specific. Talk about origin, blend, construction, vitola, strength, tasting notes, and craftsmanship. Avoid unsupported claims about provenance, age, awards, scarcity, or health.

## Web accessibility
- Maintain readable contrast; use ivory for long text on black rather than gold alone.
- Gold is an accent, not the default body-text color.
- Visible keyboard focus, meaningful alt text, reduced-motion support, and responsive navigation are required.

## Product naming system
Each cigar may be named after a historical empire or ruler, such as Caesar, Pharaoh, Tsar, Suleiman, Akbar, Napoleon, Cyrus, Genghis, Charlemagne, Saadian, Inca, and Imperator. Names are creative concepts, not verified historical provenance. Verify trademark availability and cultural sensitivity before launch.

## Asset usage
- SVG is preferred for websites and scalable artwork.
- PNG is suitable for social profiles and previews.
- Use the favicon files for browser/app icons.
- The cigar-band image is a layout template, not print-ready dieline art.
- Do not stretch, recolor, rotate, or add effects to the logo without a defined brand variation.
