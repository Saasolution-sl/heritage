# Empire Cigars — Brand Guidelines

Version 2.0 · 17 October 2026 · Supersedes the first-pass kit in `reference-kit-v1/` (kept unchanged for reference).

> **Status.** The identity, logo family, palette, bands and packaging are concept masters, ready for screen use and proofing. Before print production or trademark filing, a designer and a printer must approve final artwork, and the name and marks need a trademark clearance search in each launch market.

---

## 1. Positioning

**Brand:** EMPIRE CIGARS
**Tagline:** *A Collection of Empires. A Legacy in Every Puff.*

Empire Cigars is a contemporary luxury cigar house whose range is organised as twelve collections, each inspired by one of history's great empires, dynasties or civilisations. It should read as an international cigar house: authoritative, collectible, historically literate and calm. Never as a discount tobacconist, a gaming brand, or fantasy-themed costume.

**Attributes:** imperial luxury · tobacco craftsmanship · historical character · international prestige · refined exclusivity · heritage-inspired storytelling · distinct collection identities.

### Tone of voice

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Confident, measured, specific. | Superlatives ("the finest in the world"). |
| Talk about format, leaf, construction, band and box. | Invent provenance, age, awards, scarcity or founding dates. |
| Say plainly what is a concept and what is confirmed. | Blur concept specifications into product facts. |
| Treat history with respect: "inspired by", "in tribute to". | Imply endorsement by any state, family or faith. |
| Address adults. | Use youth-oriented language, slang, memes or lifestyle claims. |

The brand is new and says so: *"Our heritage is borrowed with respect from history; our own record begins with the first cigar we make."*

## 2. Logo family

All logo files are outlined vectors (no live text) generated by `scripts/brand/build-assets.mjs` into `public/brand/logos/`. Each layout exists in six colourways plus an "on-obsidian" version with its own background.

| Layout | File stem | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Primary | `empire-cigars-primary-*` | Crown + laurel + EC monogram + wordmark + tagline. Brand moments, footer, documents, packaging back panels. |
| Secondary wordmark | `empire-cigars-wordmark-*`, `empire-cigars-wordmark-tagline-*` | Editorial headers, long horizontal formats. |
| EC monogram | `empire-cigars-monogram-*` | Seals, embossing, favicons, small spaces. |
| Compact crest | `empire-cigars-crest-*` | Crown over monogram. Avatars, app icons, age gate, business cards. |
| Laurel crest | `empire-cigars-laurel-*` | Crown, laurel and monogram without wordmark. Box lids, wax-seal style marks. |
| Horizontal | `empire-cigars-horizontal-*` | Website header and any wide, short slot. |
| Stacked | `empire-cigars-stacked-*` | Packaging fronts, social, square formats. |

| Colourway | Suffix | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Full colour | `-full-color` | Gold mark and wordmark, antique-gold rule, ivory tagline. **Default on obsidian.** |
| Gold | `-gold` | Single colour: foil stamping, one-colour gold printing. |
| White | `-white` | Single colour on dark photography or dark accents. |
| Black | `-black` | Single colour on light grounds, blind debossing guides, fax/legal. |
| Ivory | `-ivory` | Single colour on obsidian where gold would compete. |
| On light | `-on-light` | Antique-gold mark, obsidian wordmark, tobacco tagline on ivory/paper. |

Raster exports (1200 px wide, transparent) are in `public/brand/logos/png/`.

### Construction notes

- **Crown** — refined from the supplied kit's five-point crown into a solid form with orbs and jewel cut-outs, so it survives foil stamping and 16 px rendering.
- **EC monogram** — a bold Cormorant Garamond E set tight against a lighter C. The pair reads left to right as one unit without the letters fusing. (An earlier nested "E inside C" read as "CE" and was rejected.)
- **Wordmark** — Cormorant Garamond SemiBold capitals, tracked +160.
- **Laurel** — two generated branches meeting at the base, open at the top under the crown.

### Clear space and minimum sizes

- Clear space on all sides = the height of the **E** in the monogram (cap height of the wordmark for wordmark-only layouts).
- Minimum sizes:

| Layout | Screen | Print |
|---|---|---|
| Primary | 160 px wide | 35 mm wide |
| Horizontal | 140 px wide | 30 mm wide |
| Wordmark | 120 px wide | 25 mm wide |
| Compact crest | 32 px tall | 10 mm tall |
| Monogram | 16 px | 5 mm |

Below the primary minimum, switch to the horizontal logo; below that, the crest or monogram. The tagline is dropped below 60 mm / 280 px wide.

### Incorrect usage

Do not:
1. Stretch, squash, skew or rotate the logo.
2. Recolour it outside the six colourways (in particular, never in a collection accent colour).
3. Add glows, bevels, drop shadows, gradients or textures to the logo artwork. (Real foil and embossing on print are fine.)
4. Rearrange the crown, monogram or wordmark, or set the wordmark in another typeface.
5. Place it on busy photography without a calm area or overlay.
6. Use the gold version on tobacco brown or mid-tone accents where contrast falls below 3:1 — use ivory or white.
7. Recreate the monogram from type: always use the supplied files.
8. Combine it with a collection emblem inside a single lock-up except in the approved band and box layouts.

| Name | HEX | RGB | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Obsidian Black | `#0B0907` | 11 9 7 | Main background, navigation, luxury surfaces |
| Imperial Gold | `#D6AD55` | 214 173 85 | Primary highlights, primary buttons, key branding |
| Antique Gold | `#9A7434` | 154 116 52 | Borders, ornament, rules, secondary details |
| Tobacco Brown | `#5B321E` | 91 50 30 | Textures, warm surfaces, photography |
| Warm Ivory | `#F3E7CC` | 243 231 204 | Body text on dark, editorial details |

Proportions on the website: roughly 80% obsidian, 12% ivory text, 5% gold, 3% tobacco/texture. **Gold is an accent**: headings are ivory; gold is reserved for the logo, eyebrows, key actions, numerals and fine ornament.

Print: CMYK and Pantone matches must be proofed by the printer; gold elements on bands and boxes are specified as **foil** (warm gold), not as a printed yellow.

**Refinements from the brief, and why:**
- *Caesar* moved toward a purple-leaning crimson and *Tsar* toward a brown-leaning oxblood: the brief's `#741F2B` and `#701F2B` were indistinguishable side by side.
- *Napoleon* darkened to navy so it separates from *Pharaoh*'s lapis.
- *Suleiman* (warm forest green) and *Saadian* (blue-leaning emerald) were pulled apart for the same reason.
- *Cyrus* and *Saadian* were darkened slightly so gold foil artwork on them meets 3:1 contrast (enforced by `tests/brand.test.ts`).
- *Akbar* became a mid jade carrying obsidian ink, and *Inca* a more orange gold so it does not read as Imperator.

Source of truth: `src/data/collections.json`; mirrored in `src/styles/tokens.css` and `docs/brand/tokens.json` (a test keeps CSS and data in sync).

### Accessibility and contrast

| Pair | Ratio | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Ivory on obsidian | 16.2:1 | Body text |
| Muted ivory `#C9BDA3` on obsidian | 10.7:1 | Secondary text |
| Faint ivory `#8F8572` on obsidian | 5.5:1 | Captions only |
| Gold on obsidian | 9.4:1 | Eyebrows, links, highlights |
| Obsidian on gold | 9.4:1 | Primary buttons |

Never set long text in gold. Collection accents are surfaces, not text colours.

## 4. Typography

| Role | Typeface | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Display, headings, logo | **Cormorant Garamond** (400–700, italic) | Sentence case for headings; tracked capitals only for the wordmark, band lettering and very short labels. |
| Body, UI, labels | **Inter** (300–600) | Eyebrow labels in 12–14 px capitals tracked +220. |

Both are SIL Open Font License; the website self-hosts them via `@fontsource`. Hierarchy (fluid): display 50–106 px · title 32–50 px · H3 19–23 px · lede 19–23 px light · body 16–17 px / 1.65.

## 5. Ornament, patterns and iconography

- **Ornamental rule** — a fine line broken by a gold lozenge. Use once per section head at most.
- **Imperial lattice** (`patterns/imperial-lattice-tile.svg`) — packaging linings, tissue paper, backgrounds at ≤ 15% opacity.
- **Laurel border** (`patterns/laurel-border.svg`) — certificates, box edges.
- **Saadian zellige** (`patterns/saadian-zellige-tile.svg`) — Saadian collection only.
- **Tobacco-leaf texture** (`patterns/tobacco-leaf-texture.svg`) — subtle warmth behind hero imagery.
- **Icons** — 1.5 px stroke line icons, square caps, no fills; gold only on hover/active.
- Ornament frames content; it is never the content. Generous negative space is part of the identity.

## 7. Cigar bands

Files: `public/brand/cigar-bands/{slug}-band.svg` (artwork) and `cigar-bands/print/{slug}-band-print.svg` (dieline, bleed, glue area, notes). Units are 0.1 mm.

**Layout principles**
- Central medallion (Ø 26 mm): obsidian ring with "EMPIRE CIGARS" on the top arc and the collection name on the bottom arc; inner field in the collection accent carrying the emblem.
- Wings (16 mm): obsidian with gold foil rules and a lozenge chain (eight-point stars on Saadian); a central accent stripe carries the collection numeral.
- **Flat length = π × ring-gauge diameter + 10 mm overlap**, computed per vitola (e.g. RG 52 → 74.8 mm).
- Layers named by process: `paper` (printed colour), `foil-gold`, `ink-collection` (emblem and numeral), `dieline`.

**Print constraints**
- Minimum foil line 0.3 mm. Medallion lettering has a cap height of about 1.2–1.4 mm, which is within what fine-line foil can hold but at its limit: **confirm with the printer**, and enlarge the medallion (or drop the arc text to a single line) if their minimum is higher.
- Keep text 1.5 mm inside the dieline.
- Emboss the medallion ring and emblem; deboss is not recommended on 80–90 gsm band stock.
- Final stock, foil colour and registration tolerances to be agreed with the band printer.

## 8. Packaging

Concept files: `public/brand/packaging/{slug}-box-lid.svg` (1200 × 800, lid face) and `collection-presentation-box.svg` (the twelve-collection set).

- Every lid carries: the wordmark, the collection numeral and name, the empire, the vitola line, and the collection band motif across the lid.
- Obsidian lacquer or wrapped board with gold foil frame; the accent appears as a band across the lid, echoing the cigar band.
- Materials (cedar, lacquer, leatherette) are **not** specified until sourced; do not claim them in copy.
- Regulatory panels (health warnings, tax stamps, origin, manufacturer, barcode) take priority over decoration and must be laid out per market before print. None are drawn on the concept lids.

## 9. Photography and image direction

Current site imagery is vector illustration generated to scale from the concept vitolas. Replace with photography once real cigars exist.

- Obsidian or deep tobacco grounds; a single warm key light from the upper left; gentle falloff.
- Show real wrapper texture, veins, cap construction and the band in sharp focus.
- Consistent angle sets per product: full cigar (−20°), band close-up, foot, box open, box closed.
- One collection accent per frame at most, via props or the band itself.
- No smoke clouds as decoration, no people under 25 in appearance, no lifestyle excess, no stock "luxury" clichés.
- Colour-correct to the master palette; never saturate the wrapper.
